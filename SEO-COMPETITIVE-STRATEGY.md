## 9. Content Writing Guidelines

### For each blog post:
- **Length:** 1500-2500 words minimum
- **Title format:** Question or "How to" format (matches search queries)
- **Structure:** H1 title → intro paragraph → H2 sections → H3 subsections → conclusion with CTA
- **Include:** Relevant PPC/CrPC section numbers, case law references, practical steps
- **CTA in every post:** Phone number, consultation link, chat invitation
- **Meta title:** Under 60 characters, include primary keyword + "| M Salman Tahir"
- **Meta description:** 150-160 characters, include keyword + call to action
- **Internal links:** Link to 2-3 other blog posts or pages
- **Images:** At least 1 relevant image with proper alt text per post
- **Language:** Clear, simple English. Consider bilingual (English + Urdu) sections for key posts

### SEO checklist per post:
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, first paragraph, and at least 2 H2 headings
- [ ] Meta title and meta description set in blog admin
- [ ] At least 2 internal links to other pages/posts
- [ ] Featured image with descriptive alt text
- [ ] CTA with phone number and consultation link
- [ ] Article JSON-LD structured data (automatic via SSR)
- [ ] URL slug is short, descriptive, keyword-rich
